## ⚡ Executive Summary & Technical Focus

This repository showcases an end-to-end suite of quantitative trading systems, discretionary macro execution tools, and institutional equity research. Designed for buy-side trading and financial analytics, the work bridges data science (`Python`, `GARCH`, `LSTM`, `PyTorch`) with discretionary macro trading and buy-side financial modeling.

## 🛠️ Key Projects & Research Workflows

### 📊 1. FX Implied Volatility Forecasting Engine
* **Architecture:** Hybrid GARCH(1,1) statistical time-series model paired with an LSTM neural network layer.
* **Objective:** Capture time-varying volatility clustering, non-linear market dynamics, and regime-switch detection for short-term foreign exchange implied volatility.
* **Tech Stack:** `Python`, `PyTorch`, `arch`, `pandas`, `NumPy`, `scikit-learn`

---

### 📈 2. Discretionary Macro Equity Research & Pitch Decks
* **Framework:** Conclusion-oriented stock pitches tailored for Hedge Fund Investment Committees.
* **Components:** Fully dynamic 3-statement financial models, DCF valuation, LBO modeling, and macroeconomic sensitivity analysis.
* **Key Asset:** Institutional Equity Pitch Deck & Financial Model (Nasdaq Stock Focus).
